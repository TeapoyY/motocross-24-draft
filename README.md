# 🏞 24-0: AMA Motocross Draft

**Can you sweep a 24-round outdoor motocross championship?**

X-0 Sports Draft #22 — Draft 5 AMA Motocross legends across 6 eras (1972-2026) and try to win every race of the 2026 outdoor season.

## Play

**[https://teapoyy.github.io/motocross-24-draft/](https://teapoyy.github.io/motocross-24-draft/)**

## What's inside

- **84 legends** across 6 eras (Roger DeCoster → Haiden Deegan)
- **24 races** = 12 AMA Pro Motocross outdoor rounds + 12 SMX World Championship stadium rounds
- **7 track types**: sandy, hardpack, loam, redclay, altitude, forest, stadium
- Era Synergy: bonus OVR for era cohesion (same decade = +1.5)
- Modern 25-1 points system, share-text with hashtags
